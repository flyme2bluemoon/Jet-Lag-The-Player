# Season 9: Hide + Seek

This context defines the Hide + Seek concepts modeled by the Season 9 live dashboard. Shared product language is defined in the [root context](../../../../CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Run**:
One hiding attempt, beginning when a **Player** becomes the **Hider** and ending when the **Seekers** find them.

**Hider**:
The **Player** hiding during the current **Run**.

**Seekers**:
The two **Players** who cooperate to find the **Hider** during a **Run**.

**Question**:
A request chosen from the season's set of question cards that the **Seekers** use to obtain information about the **Hider**'s location.

**Coin**:
Season currency earned by the **Hider** when they answer **Questions** and spent to buy **Curse dice**.

**Curse die**:
One of one or more dice the **Hider** purchases with **Coins** and rolls to determine which **Curse card** they receive.

**Curse card**:
A **Game card** corresponding to the total rolled on one or more **Curse dice**. The **Hider** may use it to slow the **Seekers** under the card's rules.

## Relationships

- Each **Run** has one **Hider** and two **Seekers**.
- Answering **Questions** earns **Coins** for the **Hider**.
- The **Hider** spends **Coins** on **Curse dice**, whose total determines a **Curse card**.
