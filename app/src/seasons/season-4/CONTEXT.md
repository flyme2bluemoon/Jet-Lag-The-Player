# Season 4: Battle 4 America

This context defines the Battle 4 America concepts modeled by the Season 4 live dashboard. Shared product language is defined in the [root context](../../../../CONTEXT.md).

## Language

**Challenge card**:
A **Game card** containing a challenge a **Team** can complete to claim a state.

**Hand**:
The **Challenge cards** currently held by a **Team**.

**Claimed state**:
A U.S. state or D.C. currently controlled by a **Team** after completing a **Challenge card** or winning a **Battle**. A successful defense reaffirms the defending team's control.
_Avoid_: state claim

**Battle**:
A contest in which one **Team** attacks a **Claimed state** controlled by the other team. The winner takes or retains control of the state.

**Power-up token**:
Season currency earned from some **Challenge cards** and spent on powerups.

**Travel budget**:
Each **Team**'s money for transportation.

## Relationships

- A **Team**'s **Hand** changes as it draws, completes, swaps, or discards **Challenge cards**.
- Completing a **Challenge card** may produce a **Claimed state** and may award **Power-up tokens**.
- A **Battle** may transfer a **Claimed state** to the attacking team or leave it with the defending team.
